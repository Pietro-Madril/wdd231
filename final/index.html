About Us

"Welcome, adventurer! I hope you have a wonderful stay at our humble guild. I trust you'll enjoy listening to the often overly fantastical tales of our grandmaster. If you're looking for an adventure, take a look at the quests posted on our board. I'm sure you'll find something that suits your taste." - Zéfir, the guild receptionist

The Silver Fox Guild is an original and independent project that aims to promote and bring together those who, like us, are passionate about RPGs and fantasy stories. We have several campaigns running with our game masters, who share their ideas both with the general public and with our loyal guild members.
