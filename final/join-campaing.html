About Membership

Our Membership system is simply a way to rank our campaigns. Our featured campaigns are proposed by our most experienced DMs (Dungeon Masters) and are sure to offer a deeper immersion into their narratives. It also works as a priority list, since spots in each campaign are limited.Th e Bronze Tier is the most basic, granting entry to the general public queue and access to campaign recordings. The Silver Tier offers the same benefits as the previous one, with the addition of personalized content, including visual effects for your character. The Gold Tier, the pinnacle of our campaigns, includes all previous benefits and gives you the right to receive a physical version of your character, as well as exclusive physical items from the Gold Tier campaigns you participate in. More information will be sent by email once you register.
